The image is a page from a digitised trade index of the British Chamber of Commerce for Switzerland.
Extract every company or person listed on the page.

About the source:
- The trade indexes were published between 1921 and 1958.
- A page shows an alphabetical or a thematic list of companies, in one or two columns.
- The companies are mostly located in Switzerland and the United Kingdom.
- The index is written in English and German; company names can be in English, German, French or Italian.

About the entries:
- Each entry describes a single company or person.
- Alphabetical entries have filling dots between the name and a page number. Dots and page numbers are not part of the data.
- Alphabetical entries seldom have locations; thematic entries often do.
- Thematic entries are listed under headings that describe the type of business. Headings are not entries.
- Some headings only refer to other headings, e.g. "X, s. Y". These are not entries either.

About the output:
- Return one JSON object with `page_id` and `entries`, following this example.
- `page_id` can be any identifier for the page; number `entry_id` in reading order.
- `location` is the location as written, e.g. "Zurich" or "London, UK"; null if none is given. Do not add a country that is not written with the location.
